Skinprint On-device skin read

Your photo never leaves your device — face detection and this whole read run right in your browser.

How this works

No AI service, no upload, no account. Face detection runs on-device (MediaPipe, loaded once from a CDN), then this app reads shine, color and texture patterns directly from the photo's pixels to estimate your skin type. It's a lighter-weight read than an AI vision model would give — closer to a quick visual impression than genuine reasoning — but nothing about your photo ever leaves this page, and it costs nothing to run at any scale.

Cosmetic guidance only, not a medical diagnosis. See a dermatologist about anything persistent or concerning.
