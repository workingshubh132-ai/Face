Skinprint On-device skin read

Your photo is read in this browser tab and never leaves your device — nothing is uploaded, stored, or sent to a server.

How this works

Face detection runs on-device using a model loaded once from a CDN (MediaPipe). From there, this app measures shine, color and surface texture directly from the photo's pixels and compares each face region against your own facial average — it's pixel math, not an AI model reasoning about your skin. Think quick visual impression, not lab-grade measurement. Nothing about the photo is kept once the read finishes.

Cosmetic guidance only, not a medical diagnosis. See a dermatologist about anything persistent or concerning.
