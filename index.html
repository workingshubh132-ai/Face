A considered read of your skin and hair, measured from a single photo that never leaves this page.

Nothing leaves this tab. Your photo is read here in the browser — never uploaded, never stored, never sent to a server.

How the read works

Face detection runs on-device with a model fetched once from a CDN. From there this app measures shine, colour and surface texture straight from the photo's pixels, comparing each region of your face against your own facial average. It's pixel maths, not an AI reasoning about your skin — a quick visual impression, not a lab measurement. The photo is discarded when the read finishes.
